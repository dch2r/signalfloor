signalfloor is installed. To try it from your machine:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "signalfloor.fullname" . }}-dashboard 3000:3000
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "signalfloor.fullname" . }}-api 8000:8000

Then open http://localhost:3000. Agents send traces to http://localhost:8000/v1/traces.
